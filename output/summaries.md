**Central plant - multiple — 10 Mar 2026**

What was found / done: Full plant inspection and multiple remedial actions across six assets. Attended for scheduled annual inspection across the central plant. Started at chiller CH-01: checked refrigerant charge, superheat 5K, subcool 4K, both within spec, no action. Moved to CH-02: contactor CC-1 showed pitting on contacts, replaced.
